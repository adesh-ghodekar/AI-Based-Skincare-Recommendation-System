1. Why Knowing Your Skin Type Matters

Choosing skincare without knowing your skin type is like picking shoes without knowing your size — it may not fit and could even cause problems! 💡

Your skin type determines:

  • Product Effectiveness: Ingredients that suit oily skin might irritate dry skin and vice versa.
  • Routine Customization: The right steps and frequency depend on whether your skin is dry, oily, sensitive, etc.
  • Better Results: A tailored routine ensures healthier, glowing skin!

2. Daily Routine 101 🌞🌛

Cleanser 🧼 - Remove dirt & impurities
Toner 💧 - Balance your skin's pH
Serum ✨ - Target concerns like acne or dullness
Moisturiser 🥛 - Keep skin hydrated
Sunscreen 🛡️ - Protect against sun damage

3. Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Over-exfoliating your skin
  • Skipping sunscreen on cloudy days
  • Mixing incompatible ingredients

4. Ingredient Highlights

Look for:

  • Salicylic Acid – for acne-prone skin
  • Hyaluronic Acid – for deep hydration
  • Niacinamide – to brighten and reduce pores

5. Seasonal Tips

  • Summer: Go lightweight and use more SPF.
  • Winter: Hydrate deeply and use gentle cleansers.
  • Monsoon: Avoid heavy creams, go for non-comedogenic products.

6. Lifestyle & Diet

Stay hydrated, eat a balanced diet, and sleep well. Your skin reflects your overall health!

7. Skincare Myths Busted

  • "Oily skin doesn't need moisturiser" – False!
  • "Natural means safe" – Not always!
  • "Scrubbing harder = cleaner skin" – Nope!

8. When to See a Dermatologist

If you're dealing with severe acne, persistent irritation, or sudden skin changes – it's time to consult a pro.

✨ Bonus Tip from FIORA.AI

Consistency is key! Stick to your routine, be patient, and trust the process. Beautiful skin doesn’t happen overnight, but with the right care, it *does* happen. 💜
